Employee Account Setup Guide

Do not Google Microsoft login pages. Always use the sign-in link on this page.

What You Need Before You Start

  • Your phone
  • Your work email address
  • The temporary password provided to you

You will create a new password during sign-in.

1 Install Microsoft Authenticator (Required)

Microsoft Authenticator is required to finish signing in.
Install it on your phone before continuing.

iPhone (App Store) Android (Google Play)

You will use this app to approve your sign-in.

Screenshot: Microsoft Authenticator app listing

2 Sign In to Your Work Account

Use this link. Do not use bookmarks. Do not use personal Microsoft accounts.

Sign in to your work account
Screenshot: Microsoft sign-in page

3 Enter Your Work Email

Your work email looks like this:

firstname.lastname@advantxhomecare.com

Type it exactly as given to you.

If you see a personal email or another company name, stop.
Click "Use a different account" or "Sign out" and start again.
Screenshot: Email entry screen

4 Create Your New Password

Enter the temporary password you were given.

You will be prompted to create a new password. Choose something you will remember.

Screenshot: Password creation screen

5 Approve the Sign-In in Microsoft Authenticator

Microsoft will ask you to approve the sign-in on your phone.

  1. Open the Microsoft Authenticator app.
  2. Tap Approve.

This is expected. This is not an error.

Screenshot: Authenticator approval prompt

6 Install Outlook and Teams

After you are signed in, install these apps on your phone.

Microsoft Outlook

iPhone (App Store) Android (Google Play)

Microsoft Teams

iPhone (App Store) Android (Google Play)

Sign in using your work email and the password you just created.

7 Finding Payroll After You Sign In

Payroll is accessed from inside your work account.

  1. Open Microsoft 365.
  2. Go to the Employee Portal.
  3. Look for Payroll.

For security reasons, payroll is not linked on this public page.

Screenshot: Internal SharePoint page with Payroll link highlighted

If You See "User Not Found"

This usually means:

  • The wrong login page was used
  • A personal Microsoft account was selected
  • The email address was typed incorrectly

Go back to Step 2 and use the sign-in link on this page.

Still Stuck?
