Welcome to Advantx Home Care

Compassionate, high-quality care for patients begins with caring for you and your family. This guide walks you through a simple, one-time setup for your work email, Teams, and pay information.

1. Start here

  1. Open this page from your work device by scanning the QR code or tapping the link sent to you.
  2. Have your work email address and the temporary password provided by HR ready.

If you ever get stuck, contact your supervisor or the office for help. Do not share your password over text or email.

2. Sign in to your work Microsoft account

  1. In your browser (Edge, Chrome, Safari), go to:
    https://portal.office.com
  2. Sign in with your work email address (not a personal email).
  3. Enter the temporary password you were given.
  4. When asked, create a new password you will remember and keep it private.
See the wrong company name?

If you see a different organization at the top, sign out, close the browser, and try again using the link above. Make sure you are using your work email, not a personal Microsoft account.

(Screenshot placeholder: login screen showing the correct company name/logo.)

3. Set up the Authenticator app (MFA)

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) helps keep your account secure. You will approve sign-ins using the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone.

Required Install Microsoft Authenticator on your phone:

• iPhone (App Store): Microsoft Authenticator
• Android (Google Play): Microsoft Authenticator
  1. On your computer, continue the sign-in process until it asks you to set up the Authenticator app.
  2. On your phone, open the Authenticator app and choose Add work or school account.
  3. Use the app to scan the QR code shown on your computer screen.
  4. Approve the test notification on your phone when prompted.
(Screenshot placeholder: example MFA approval notification on a phone.)

4. Install the apps you will use every day

After your first sign-in, install these apps on your phone so you can stay connected.

Recommended Teams and Outlook mobile apps:

• Microsoft Teams – iPhone: App Store link
• Microsoft Teams – Android: Google Play link
• Microsoft Outlook – iPhone: App Store link
• Microsoft Outlook – Android: Google Play link

Sign in to each app with your work email and the new password you created in step 2.

5. Find your internal site, forms, and payroll link

  1. From the Microsoft 365 home page, open the app launcher (the 9-dot grid in the top-left corner).
  2. Select your company’s Employee Portal or Home site.
  3. On that page, look for a tile or link labeled something like Payroll & Pay Stubs or HR & Benefits.
(Screenshot placeholder: internal SharePoint home page highlighting the Payroll / Employee Portal area.)

For security, this guide does not link directly to payroll or HR systems. Always access them from the internal portal after you sign in.

6. Need help?

If you cannot sign in, are locked out, or your screen looks very different from what is shown here:

  • Contact your supervisor or office support.
  • Mention where in this guide you are stuck (for example: "Step 2, changing my password").

Never send passwords, security codes, or screenshots of your full screen to anyone you do not recognize.
