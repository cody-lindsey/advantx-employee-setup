Employee Account Setup Guide

Only looking for payroll?    Skip to Step 8

✅ Recommended: Use a laptop or desktop computer for your first sign-in. This makes setting up Microsoft Authenticator faster and avoids app conflicts.
You can use your phone after your account is set up.
Do not Google Microsoft login pages. Always start with the sign-in link on this page.

What You Need Before You Start

  • A computer (laptop or desktop)
  • Your phone (for Microsoft Authenticator)
  • Your work email address
  • The temporary password provided to you

You will create a new password during sign-in.

How this works:
Use a computer to sign in.
Use your phone to install apps and approve sign-ins.

📱 Scan to View This Guide on Your Phone

You can follow the steps on your phone, but complete the first sign-in on a computer.

1 Install Microsoft Authenticator (Required)

Microsoft Authenticator is required to finish signing in.
Install it on your phone before continuing.

You'll also use your phone later to install Outlook and Teams.

You will use this app to approve your sign-in.

2 Sign In to Your Work Account

💻 On your computer: Click the Microsoft Sign In button below.

Use this link. Do not use bookmarks. Do not use personal Microsoft accounts.

📱 If you try to sign in on a phone:

Open this page in a private/incognito browser and do not use the Microsoft 365 app for the first sign-in.

Microsoft Sign In

https://login.microsoftonline.com/?whr=advantxhomecare.com&prompt=login

You may be asked to confirm switching accounts if this device has a personal Microsoft account. Choose Continue.

Sign-in screen:

3 Enter Your Work Email

Your work email looks like this:

firstname.lastname@advantxhomecare.com

Type it exactly as given to you.

If you see a personal email or another company name, stop.
Click "Use a different account" or "Sign out" and start again.

If You See "User Not Found"

This usually means:

  • The wrong login page was used
  • A personal Microsoft account was selected
  • The email address was typed incorrectly

Go back to Step 2 and use the sign-in link on this page.

4 Create Your New Password

Enter the temporary password you were given.

You will be prompted to create a new password. Choose something you will remember.

This happens before Microsoft Authenticator is used.

5 Set Up Microsoft Authenticator (First-Time Setup)

Because this is your first sign-in, Microsoft needs to connect your account to the Authenticator app. This only happens once.

On your computer, you will see a message like:
"More information required"

On your phone:

  1. Open the Microsoft Authenticator app
  2. Tap the + to add account
  3. Select Work or school account
  4. Choose Scan a QR code

On your computer:
A QR code will appear on the screen.

Use your phone to scan the QR code shown on your computer.

This is normal.
You do not need a QR code ahead of time.

6 Approve the Sign-In (Number Matching)

After your account is added, Microsoft will ask you to confirm the sign-in.

  1. Look at the number shown on your computer screen
  2. Open Microsoft Authenticator on your phone
  3. Enter the number
  4. Tap Approve or Yes

This is expected. This is not an error.

7 Install Outlook and Teams

After you are signed in, install these apps on your phone (and tablet, if you use one).

Microsoft Outlook

Microsoft Teams

Sign in using your work email and the password you just created.

Note: If signing in on a tablet, be sure to approve with your phone's Microsoft Authenticator app.

8 Finding Payroll After You Sign In

Payroll is accessed from inside your work account.

  1. Open Microsoft 365.
  2. Go to the Employee Portal.
  3. Look for Payroll.

For security reasons, payroll is not linked on this public page.
