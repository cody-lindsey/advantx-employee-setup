# Advantx Home Care – Employee Account Setup

This guide will get you logged into your work account.  
Follow the steps in order. Do not skip ahead.

**Do not Google Microsoft login pages.**  
**Always start here.**

---

## What You Need Before You Start

- Your phone
- Your work email address
- The temporary password provided to you

You will create a new password during sign-in.

---

## Step 1: Install Microsoft Authenticator (Required)

Microsoft Authenticator is required to finish signing in.  
Install it on your phone **before** continuing.

**iPhone:**  
[Download Microsoft Authenticator from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/microsoft-authenticator/id983156458)

**Android:**  
[Download Microsoft Authenticator from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.azure.authenticator)

You will use this app to approve your sign-in.

*(Screenshot: Microsoft Authenticator app listing)*

---

## Step 2: Sign In to Your Work Account

Use this link. Do not use bookmarks. Do not use personal Microsoft accounts.

**[Sign in to your work account](https://myapplications.microsoft.com/)**

*(Screenshot: Microsoft sign-in page showing "Sign in" with email field)*

---

## Step 3: Enter Your Work Email

Your work email looks like this:

`firstname.lastname@advantxhomecare.com`

Type it exactly as given to you.

If you see a personal email or another company name listed, **stop**.  
Click "Use a different account" or "Sign out" and start again.

*(Screenshot: email entry screen with example work email)*

---

## Step 4: Create Your New Password

Enter the temporary password you were given.  
You will be prompted to create a new password.

Choose something you will remember.  
Follow the on-screen requirements.

*(Screenshot: password creation screen)*

---

## Step 5: Approve the Sign-In in Microsoft Authenticator

Microsoft will ask you to approve the sign-in on your phone.

1. Open the Microsoft Authenticator app.
2. Tap **Approve**.

This is expected. This is not an error.

*(Screenshot: "More information required" screen)*  
*(Screenshot: Authenticator approval prompt on phone)*

---

## Step 6: Install Outlook and Teams

After you are signed in, install these apps on your phone.

**Microsoft Outlook**  
- [iPhone (App Store)](https://apps.apple.com/app/microsoft-outlook/id951937596)  
- [Android (Google Play)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.microsoft.office.outlook)

**Microsoft Teams**  
- [iPhone (App Store)](https://apps.apple.com/app/microsoft-teams/id1113153706)  
- [Android (Google Play)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.microsoft.teams)

Sign in using your work email and the password you just created.

---

## Step 7: Finding Payroll After You Sign In

Payroll is accessed from inside your work account.

After signing in:

1. Open Microsoft 365.
2. Go to the Employee Portal.
3. Look for **Payroll**.

For security reasons, payroll is not linked on this public page.

*(Screenshot: internal SharePoint page with Payroll link highlighted)*

---

## If You See "User Not Found"

This usually means:

- The wrong login page was used
- A personal Microsoft account was selected
- The email address was typed incorrectly

**Go back to Step 2** and use the sign-in link on this page.

---
